How it works

Four steps from blank canvas to optimized structure.

1

Model

Define geometry with parametric grids or free-form drawing. Assign sections from the AISC/European catalog.

2

Apply gravity, wind (ASCE 7-22), live, crane, and thermal loads. Auto-generate combinations.

3

Analyze & Check

4

Optimize & Build

Auto-size for minimum weight, generate reports, and push to fabrication management.
