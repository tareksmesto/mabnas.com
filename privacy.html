1. Introduction

Mabna ("we", "us", "our") is a structural steel design and fabrication management platform operated under the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data in compliance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) (Royal Decree M/19, dated 9/2/1443H) and its implementing regulations issued by the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA).

By using our website (mabnas.com), platform (app.mabnas.com), or contacting us, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy. This policy is available in both English and Arabic. In case of conflict, the Arabic version prevails.

4. Legal basis for processing (PDPL Art. 5–6)

We process your personal data on one or more of the following grounds:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)) — When you submit the contact form or use the AI assistant, you consent to our processing your data for that purpose. Consent is freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous.
  • Contract performance (Art. 6(2)) — When you create an account, we process your data to provide the services under our Terms of Service.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(5)) — Security logging, error monitoring, and rate limiting to protect the platform and its users. We balance our interest against your rights and only process the minimum data necessary.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(3)) — Where required to comply with Saudi laws and regulations.

5. How we use your data

Cross-border transfers (PDPL Art. 29)

Your core data (account, projects, models, fabrication records) is stored and processed within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (OCI Jeddah region).

The only cross-border transfer occurs when you choose to use the AI assistant feature. When you send a message to the AI assistant, that message and relevant model context are sent to Anthropic's API servers in the United States for processing. This transfer is based on:

  • Your explicit consent — You initiate each AI interaction voluntarily. The AI assistant is an optional feature.
  • Contractual safeguards — Anthropic's API terms prohibit using customer data for model training (zero-retention API).
  • Data minimization — Only the data necessary for the specific AI query is transmitted; we do not send your full database.

If you do not wish your data to be transferred outside KSA, do not use the AI assistant feature. All other platform functions operate entirely within KSA.

7. AI-powered features (PDPL Art. 10)

Transparency notice: The Mabna platform includes an AI-powered engineering assistant. This section explains how it works and what data is involved.

What the AI assistant does:

  • Answers engineering questions about your model and design results
  • Assists with interpreting design code provisions (AISC, ASCE)
  • Helps navigate the platform and automate repetitive tasks

How it works:

  • When you type a message, it is sent to Anthropic's Claude API (a large language model) along with relevant context about your current model.
  • Anthropic processes the request and returns a response. Under their API terms, your data is not used to train their models and is not retained beyond the request lifecycle.
  • Your conversation history is stored in a separate database on our KSA server for your convenience (so you can see past conversations).

Automated decision-making (PDPL Art. 10):

  • The AI assistant provides suggestions and information only. It does not make binding decisions about your structural designs.
  • All AI outputs must be reviewed and validated by a qualified engineer before being relied upon.
  • The AI does not determine your account status, pricing, access level, or any other outcome with legal effect.
  • You have the right to request human review of any AI-generated recommendation.

Your choices:

  • The AI assistant is optional. You can use all other platform features without it.
  • You control what you send to the AI — do not include personal data of third parties in your messages.
  • You may request deletion of your AI conversation history at any time.

9. Data security (PDPL Art. 19)

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in transit: TLS 1.2+ everywhere (HTTPS, HSTS preload, secure cookies)
  • Encryption at rest: OCI block volume encryption for all stored data
  • Authentication: Keycloak (OIDC/PKCE, RS256 JWT, brute-force lockout, optional TOTP/MFA)
  • Access control: Role-based permissions with deny-by-default organization isolation — no company can see another's data
  • Rate limiting: 300/min global, 10/min on compute endpoints, 5/min on contact form
  • Password security: PBKDF2 hashing, temporary passwords forced to change on first login
  • Audit trail: Hash-chained fabrication audit log, model edit traceability
  • Error handling: PII scrubbed from error reports (send_default_pii=False)
  • Infrastructure: Private Docker network; only the reverse proxy is exposed to the internet

10. Data breach notification (PDPL Art. 20)

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to cause serious harm to data subjects:

  • We will notify SDAIA within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.
  • We will notify affected data subjects without undue delay if the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms.
  • Notification will include: the nature of the breach, categories of data affected, likely consequences, and measures taken or proposed to mitigate the breach.
  • We maintain an internal breach register documenting all incidents, their effects, and remedial actions taken.
